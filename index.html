Frequently asked questions

Do you take commissions?

Yes! I take commissions for posters and video edits. Message me on Discord or email me with what you need, your deadline and your budget, and I'll reply with a quote.

What do you need from me for a poster?

Your idea, any text you want on it, the Minecraft skins of the players to feature, and your logo or colours if you have them. I'll handle the scene, render and design.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the job. I'll give you a timeline before I start, so you'll know exactly when to expect it.

How do I join Tokyo Core SMP?

Connect to play.tokyocoresmp.xyz in Minecraft and join the Discord at discord.gg/tokyocoresmp for events, updates and announcements.
